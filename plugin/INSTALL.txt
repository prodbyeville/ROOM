room 1.0
========

INSTALL (Windows, VST3)
1. Close your DAW.
2. Run "ROOM 1.0.0 Setup.exe". It puts ROOM.vst3 in
       C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
   This build isn't signed yet, so Windows may say "Windows protected your
   PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   (Prefer to copy it by hand? The ROOM.vst3 folder is in the download too:
   copy it into the folder above.)
3. Open your DAW and rescan plugins if it doesn't find ROOM by itself.
   - FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find more plugins
   - Ableton: Preferences > Plug-ins > Rescan
4. Look for "ROOM" by "Eville" under effects.

INSTALL (Mac, AU + VST3, Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 10.13 or later)
1. Close your DAW.
2. Open "ROOM <version>.pkg". This build isn't signed by Apple yet, so the
   first time macOS says it can't be checked: right-click the .pkg, choose
   Open, then Open again (or System Settings > Privacy & Security >
   Open Anyway).
3. The installer puts ROOM.component in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
   (Logic, GarageBand) and ROOM.vst3 in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
   (Ableton, FL Studio, Studio One...). The "plugins" folder has both too,
   if you'd rather copy them by hand.
4. Open your DAW. Logic checks new plugins when it starts; others may need
   a rescan. Look for "ROOM" by "Eville" under effects.

USE
1. Put ROOM on a track (a one-shot, a melody, anything).
2. Drag any sound onto the dashed box at the top, or click it to browse.
   A clap in a room, tape hiss, a kick, found sound...
3. ROOM listens to that sound and builds a room from it.

LAYOUT
ROOM is one unit with three panels:

  the console (dust white, top left)
    top        the menu (three lines): new preset (back to init, the room
               stays), tips on / off, check for updates, visit website,
               get support, about, the version.
               the studios window ([heart] name < >), save,
               randomize (right-click to choose what moves), advanced (opens
               the advanced section on the right; the window grows to fit),
               wet, out, and bypass (the key glows red while ROOM is on)
    load       the loaded room and its fingerprint (lows at the bottom,
               highs at the top, time to the right: each band fades as
               it dies away). click it for the ROOMS library, drop a sound
               on it to build a room, right-click for more (sound file,
               clear). record: play / clap / sing into the track, press
               again to stop (max 30 s). save: keep this room as a ROOM.
    room       the room itself: a box in 3D, turning slowly. it moves with
               the knobs: space = width, size = depth and height,
               intensity = glow, tail = how far the decay reaches across
               the floor, damping = colour, pre-delay = gap, ducking and
               incoming sound = pulse, freeze = stops it and holds.
               click it for the room's numbers.
    tone       the tone the room puts on a sound (the red dot = its
               strongest resonance)
    knobs      intensity, balance (+ auto), tail, space

  advanced (black, the right side; open it with the advanced key)
    pre-delay, size, damping, ducking, release, freeze
    lfo 1 / lfo 2: click a card to open both lfos, stacked (shape, rate or
    sync, retrig, what it moves). right-click a knob for its modulation. drag a card or the lfo handle
    onto any knob to assign it. a dot appears next to the knob: drag it
    up / down for the amount, double-click to remove. "done" goes back.

  finish (black, along the bottom)
    eq (drag the 5 bands, scroll = width, double-click = reset), tape,
    auto eq, the power light and the out jack

The window can be resized from the bottom-right corner.

SAVING: ROOMS and STUDIOS
  There are two things you can save:

  ROOM     just the room: its decay per band, tone, resonances and balance
           (what makes that space unique). No knobs. Loading a ROOM swaps the
           room and keeps all your knobs and LFOs.
           Save / load with the two icons in tier 1, next to Record.
           Tags: Type and Character (you pick), plus Decay (Short / Medium /
           Long), Tone (Dark / Neutral / Bright) and Source (Factory / File /
           Recorded), which ROOM works out by itself.

  STUDIO   the whole sound: the room plus every knob and the LFOs.
           The bar at the top: click it to open the browser.
           < >    step through STUDIOS (in the browser's current list/order)
           Heart  favorite the current STUDIO
           Save   save the current sound as a STUDIO
           Tags: Made for (Vocals, Drums, Keys, Guitar, Bass, Synth,
           Bus/Master, FX) and Style. A STUDIO also remembers its room's name.
           Output, Bypass and Freeze are never saved in a STUDIO.

  Both browsers: search, filter by pack and tags, click a column to sort,
  heart to favorite, double-click to load. Your own can be edited (rename,
  retag, move pack) or deleted.

  Where they live:  Documents\Eville\ROOMS    (.room files)
                    Documents\Eville\STUDIOS  (.studio files)
  Each folder in there is a pack. Copy files in or out to share them, or use
  Import... / Export... in the browser.
  Presets you saved with an earlier build are moved over automatically: each
  one becomes a STUDIO, and its room becomes a ROOM with the same name.

Note: Auto EQ adds about 43 ms of latency, which your DAW compensates for
automatically (plugin delay compensation).

The analysed room is saved with your project, so you don't need to keep the
original sound file around.

If the plugin doesn't show up after a rescan, Windows may have blocked the
downloaded zip: right-click the zip > Properties > tick "Unblock" > OK, then
extract and copy again.

UPDATES
  ROOM checks once a day (quietly, in the background) for
  https://prodbyeville.com/room/latest.json, a tiny file like:

      { "version": "0.2.0", "url": "https://prodbyeville.com/room" }

  If "version" is newer than the installed ROOM, a red dot appears on the
  menu key and the menu shows "update available" (it opens "url").
  No file, no internet: nothing happens.

TIPS AND THE TOUR
  Rest the mouse on any knob or key for a moment to see what it does.
  Turn tips off (or back on) in the menu.
  The first time ROOM opens, a three-step tour shows load, shape and
  finish. See it again any time: menu > show the tour.

FIRST RUN
  A new ROOM starts with "Init Room" loaded (a plain, short room), so you
  hear it straight away. Projects you saved keep whatever they had.

FACTORY LIBRARY
  49 ROOMS (spaces, objects and textures) and 46 STUDIOS, covering every
  "made for" and character tag. 24 ROOMS come from sounds synthesized for
  ROOM (plugin/Tools/make_factory_presets.sh) and 25 from Eville's own
  recordings (plugin/Tools/make_pack_rooms.sh). A ROOM keeps only the
  analysis, never the audio.
