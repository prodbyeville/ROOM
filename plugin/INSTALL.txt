ROOM (working name) - Windows VST3, prototype build
===================================================

INSTALL
1. Close your DAW.
2. Copy the "ROOM.vst3" folder into:
       C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
   (Windows will ask for admin permission. Click Continue.)
3. Open your DAW and rescan plugins if it doesn't find ROOM by itself.
   - FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find more plugins
   - Ableton: Preferences > Plug-ins > Rescan
4. Look for "ROOM" by "Eville" under effects.

USE
1. Put ROOM on a track (a one-shot, a melody, anything).
2. Drag any sound onto the dashed box at the top, or click it to browse.
   A clap in a room, tape hiss, a kick, found sound...
3. ROOM listens to that sound and builds a room from it.

LAYOUT (top to bottom)
  Top bar    STUDIOS bar, heart, Save | Advanced, Randomize,
             Dry/Wet, Out, Bypass
  1 LOAD     drop a sound (or click to browse), or press the red record
             button and play / clap / sing into the track (press again to
             stop, max 30 s): that becomes the room.
             Folder icon = load a saved ROOM, arrow icon = save this room
             as a ROOM
  2 ROOM     what ROOM measured (decay per band, tone), then
             Intensity  how strongly the room's character is applied
             Balance    tone (resonances + EQ) vs space (tail). "Auto" uses
                        what ROOM detected from your sound
             Tail       maximum reverb length
             Space      stereo width (left = mono, middle = untouched,
                        right = wide; the lows stay tight as it widens)
  3 FINISH   EQ display (drag the 5 bands, scroll = width, double-click =
             reset), Tape, Auto EQ

TOP BAR
  Advanced   swaps the bottom section for the ADVANCED screen (below)
  Randomize  new random knob settings in musical ranges. Keeps your room,
             Dry/Wet, Out, Bypass and Freeze. Right-click it to choose what
             gets randomized: room knobs, Advanced, EQ bands, Tape + Auto EQ,
             LFO settings (off by default)
  Dry/Wet    blend of your original sound and the room
  Out        output volume
  Bypass     turns ROOM off (no timing shift)

ADVANCED SCREEN
  Pre-delay  gap before the tail starts (keeps vocals/drums punchy)
  Size       stretches or shrinks the whole decay (50% - 200%)
  Damping    + = darker tail (highs die faster), - = brighter tail
  Ducking    the room dips while you play and swells back in the gaps
  Release    how fast the room comes back after ducking
  Freeze     holds the current tail forever (pads, transitions)
  Below the Advanced knobs are two tabs:
  MODULATION two LFOs (this tab opens first). Drag an LFO's handle onto any
             knob to make it move that knob. A coloured ring shows how far
             it swings it, and a small dot appears next to the knob: drag
             the dot up/down to set the amount (Shift = fine), double-click
             it to remove. Each LFO also lists the knobs it moves as chips
             you can drag the same way.
             Right-click any knob to add an LFO, pick a depth (10-100%),
             invert it or remove it.
             Clear LFOs removes every assignment and resets both LFOs (your
             knobs stay where they are).
             Shape: sine, triangle, saw up/down, square, S&H, smooth random
             Rate in Hz, or Sync to the song tempo (1/32 note .. 8 bars)
             Retrig restarts the LFO when you press play
             The knob itself never moves, so automation still works.
             LFO assignments are saved in your project and in STUDIOS.
  ROOM INFO  everything ROOM measured: balance, decay per band (measured
             and as your knobs set it now), resonances, length

The window can be resized from the bottom-right corner.

SAVING: ROOMS and STUDIOS
  There are two things you can save:

  ROOM     just the room: its decay per band, tone, resonances and balance
           (what makes that space unique). No knobs. Loading a ROOM swaps the
           room and keeps all your knobs and LFOs.
           Save / load with the two icons in tier 1, next to Record.
           Tags: Type and Character (you pick), plus Decay (Short / Medium /
           Long), Tone (Dark / Neutral / Bright) and Source (Factory / File /
           Recorded), which ROOM works out by itself.

  STUDIO   the whole sound: the room plus every knob and the LFOs.
           The bar at the top: click it to open the browser.
           < >    step through STUDIOS (in the browser's current list/order)
           Heart  favorite the current STUDIO
           Save   save the current sound as a STUDIO
           Tags: Made for (Vocals, Drums, Keys, Guitar, Bass, Synth,
           Bus/Master, FX) and Style. A STUDIO also remembers its room's name.
           Output, Bypass and Freeze are never saved in a STUDIO.

  Both browsers: search, filter by pack and tags, click a column to sort,
  heart to favorite, double-click to load. Your own can be edited (rename,
  retag, move pack) or deleted.

  Where they live:  Documents\Eville\ROOMS    (.room files)
                    Documents\Eville\STUDIOS  (.studio files)
  Each folder in there is a pack. Copy files in or out to share them, or use
  Import... / Export... in the browser.
  Presets you saved with an earlier build are moved over automatically: each
  one becomes a STUDIO, and its room becomes a ROOM with the same name.

Note: Auto EQ adds about 43 ms of latency, which your DAW compensates for
automatically (plugin delay compensation).

The analysed room is saved with your project, so you don't need to keep the
original sound file around.

If the plugin doesn't show up after a rescan, Windows may have blocked the
downloaded zip: right-click the zip > Properties > tick "Unblock" > OK, then
extract and copy again.
