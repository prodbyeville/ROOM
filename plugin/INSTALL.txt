ROOM (working name) - Windows VST3, prototype build
===================================================

INSTALL
1. Close your DAW.
2. Copy the "ROOM.vst3" folder into:
       C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
   (Windows will ask for admin permission. Click Continue.)
3. Open your DAW and rescan plugins if it doesn't find ROOM by itself.
   - FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find more plugins
   - Ableton: Preferences > Plug-ins > Rescan
4. Look for "ROOM" by "Eville" under effects.

USE
1. Put ROOM on a track (a one-shot, a melody, anything).
2. Drag any sound onto the dashed box at the top, or click it to browse.
   A clap in a room, tape hiss, a kick, found sound...
3. ROOM listens to that sound and builds a room from it.

KNOBS
  Intensity  how strongly the room's character is applied
             (0% = none, 100% = as measured, 200% = exaggerated)
  Mix        dry vs processed
  Balance    tone (resonances + EQ) vs space (reverb tail).
             "Auto" uses what ROOM detected from your sound.
  Tail       maximum reverb length
  Space      stereo width. Left = mono, middle = untouched, right = extra
             wide (the low end stays tight as it widens)
  Output     output volume

EQ + TONE (bottom section, applied after the room)
  EQ display shows what's playing. Drag the 5 numbered bands for small cuts
             and boosts (+/-12 dB). Scroll on a band to make it wider or
             narrower. Double-click a band to reset it.
  Tape       low cut and high cut that close in as you turn it up.
             0% = off, 100% = only about 80 Hz to 9 kHz left (worn tape).
  Auto EQ    listens live and pulls down harsh, ringing frequencies only when
             they poke out (mostly 2-10 kHz). The red area on the display
             shows what it is cutting right now. 0% = off.

ROOMS (presets)
  The bar at the top shows the current ROOM. Click it to open the browser.
  < >        step through ROOMS (in the browser's current list and order)
  Heart      favorite the current ROOM
  Save       save the current sound as a ROOM: name, type, styles, pack, notes
  Browser    search, filter by pack / type / style, click a column to sort,
             heart to favorite, double-click to load. Your own ROOMS can be
             edited (rename, retag, move pack) or deleted.
  A ROOM stores the analyzed room and every knob except Output.

  Your ROOMS live in:  Documents\Eville\ROOMS
  Each folder in there is a pack. Copy .room files in or out to share them,
  or use Import... / Export... in the browser.

Note: Auto EQ adds about 43 ms of latency, which your DAW compensates for
automatically (plugin delay compensation).

The analysed room is saved with your project, so you don't need to keep the
original sound file around.

If the plugin doesn't show up after a rescan, Windows may have blocked the
downloaded zip: right-click the zip > Properties > tick "Unblock" > OK, then
extract and copy again.
