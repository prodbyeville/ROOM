ROOM {VERSION} for Mac
==================

In this folder
  ROOM-{VERSION}-macOS.pkg   the installer (AU + VST3, Apple Silicon and Intel)
  ROOM Guide.pdf             every control, explained
  Read Me First.txt          this file

Install
  1. Close your DAW.
  2. Right-click ROOM-{VERSION}-macOS.pkg and choose Open, then Open again.
     ROOM is new and not notarized yet, so macOS asks first. If it still
     won't open, go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and click
     Open Anyway.
  3. Follow the steps. ROOM goes into /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins
     (Components for AU, VST3 for VST3).
  4. Open your DAW. Logic and GarageBand check new Audio Units on launch;
     in other DAWs, rescan your plugins.
  5. Load ROOM on a track. It's listed under Eville.

Sign in
  ROOM is free. The first time it opens, sign in with your email and the
  license key from your ROOM email (no key yet? click "get room free").
  You need the internet once; after that ROOM works offline. One license
  works on up to 3 computers.

Your rooms and studios
  Documents/Eville/ROOMS and Documents/Eville/STUDIOS, one folder per pack.

Updates are free: ROOM shows a red dot on its menu when one is out.
