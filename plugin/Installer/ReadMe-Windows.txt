ROOM {VERSION} for Windows
======================

In this folder
  ROOM-{VERSION}-Windows-Setup.exe   the installer
  ROOM Guide.pdf                     every control, explained
  Read Me First.txt                  this file

Install
  1. Close your DAW.
  2. Double-click ROOM-{VERSION}-Windows-Setup.exe.
     If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then
     Run anyway. ROOM is new and not code-signed yet; the file is safe.
  3. Accept the license, click Next, then Install. ROOM goes into
     C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3.
  4. Open your DAW and rescan your plugins:
       FL Studio:    Options > Manage plugins > Find more plugins
       Ableton Live: Settings > Plug-Ins > Rescan
       Most others find it the next time they open.
  5. Load ROOM on a track. It's listed under Eville.

Sign in
  ROOM is free. The first time it opens, sign in with your email and the
  license key from your ROOM email (no key yet? click "get room free").
  You need the internet once; after that ROOM works offline. One license
  works on up to 3 computers.

Your rooms and studios
  Documents\Eville\ROOMS and Documents\Eville\STUDIOS, one folder per pack.

Updates are free: ROOM shows a red dot on its menu when one is out.
