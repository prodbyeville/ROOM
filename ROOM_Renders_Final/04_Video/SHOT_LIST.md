# ROOM video shot list

**Method (locked on clip 01, v7b):**
1. **First frame:** your original render.
2. **Last frame:** built locally with a real change of viewpoint (truck, arc or rise via perspective warp, never a plain crop), with the focus set on the end subject.
3. **Model:** Kling 3.0 Pro, sound off, 5 s.
4. **Post:** a free depth-of-field pass, then a conform to 1920x1080 at 24 fps with no audio.

Nothing on the module moves. Only the camera and the focus do.

**Cost:** about 8.75 credits per clip.
